Privacy Policy for ido

Last Updated: February 2026

Introduction

Welcome to ido, a task management application developed by Sarthak Ghosh. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Summary: Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information with anyone.

Data Storage

All your tasks and app data are stored locally on your device in a file called ido-data.json. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to sync
  • You have complete control over your information
  • Deleting the app removes all associated data

Google OAuth Authentication

ido uses Google OAuth for optional Google Drive sync functionality. When you choose to sign in with Google:

  • We only request access to create and manage the ido-data.json file in your Google Drive
  • We access your basic profile information (name and email) solely for display purposes within the app
  • We do not access any other files in your Google Drive
  • You can revoke access at any time through your Google Account settings

What We Don't Do

We are committed to your privacy. Here's what we explicitly do not do:

  • No selling of data: We never sell your personal information to third parties
  • No third-party analytics: We do not use analytics services that track your behavior
  • No tracking: We do not track your location, usage patterns, or browsing activity
  • No advertising: We do not display ads or share data with advertisers
  • No data collection: We do not collect or store your data on our servers

Data Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's security settings. We recommend:

  • Using a strong device passcode or biometric authentication
  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Using the optional Google Drive sync for backup purposes

Children's Privacy

ido does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is intended for general audiences and does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
